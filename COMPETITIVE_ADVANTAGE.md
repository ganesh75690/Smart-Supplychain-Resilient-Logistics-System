### 5. Explainable AI - Trust Through Transparency

**Traditional Platforms:**
- Black-box AI with no reasoning
- "The algorithm says so" explanations
- No confidence metrics

**SmartChain AI:**
- **Every prediction includes:**
  - **Confidence score** (0-100%)

  - **Impact metrics** - Expected cost/time savings
  - **Priority level** - High/Medium/Low urgency
- **SHAP values** for feature importance
- **Decision logging** for audit trails

**Example:**
```
Recommendation: Reroute Truck-7 via Highway 280
Confidence: 94%
Reasoning: Real-time traffic analysis shows 40% slower speeds on 
current route. Historical data indicates alternative saves avg 
2.1 hours during peak times.
Impact: Save 2h 15m, ₹85 fuel cost
Priority: High
```

**Impact:** Build trust in AI decisions through complete transparency and explainability.
